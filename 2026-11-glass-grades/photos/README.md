The eight photographs in this folder, and their thumbnails in `thumbs/`, are
mine, seven taken with my X-T50 and one with my phone. They are here so the glass grades study has real
pictures to grade, and they are shown on its page, on its card on the index
and in this repository for that alone.

They are not covered by the repository's MIT licence, which is for the code.
Without my written permission they may not be copied, edited, redistributed,
published anywhere else, or used to train AI.

One of them, the storm, is also on the study's page in the companion Figma
file, and Figma Community publishes a free file under CC BY 4.0, so the copy
there may be reused with credit. The other seven are not in the file.

Copying the study's folder out takes the code with it: leave this folder
behind and give the component a picture of your own, picked, dropped or
pasted, or point its markup at one.

Each file says the same in its own metadata, in EXIF, IPTC and XMP, with the
IPTC data-mining field set to prohibited, so the notice goes where the file
goes.
